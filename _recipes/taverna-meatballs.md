---
title: Taverna Meatballs
description: Greek taverna-style meatballs with oregano and parsley, kept light with a splash of soda water.
rest: At least 1 h in the fridge
tags: [greek, mains]
ingredients:
  - 500 g ground beef
  - ½ onion, grated
  - 7 g salt
  - 1 egg
  - 10 g parsley
  - 50 g soda water
  - 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  - 50 g breadcrumbs
  - 1 tsp oregano
  - A little black pepper
---

## Method

1. Knead all the ingredients together.
2. Cover the bowl with cling film and refrigerate for **at least 1 hour**.
3. Oil the meatballs and cook in a frying pan or on a griddle over **medium to high heat** until browned on all sides.
