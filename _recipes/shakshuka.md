---
title: Shakshuka
description: Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. One pan, bread for mopping.
time: 35 min
serves: 2–3
tags: [breakfast, vegetarian, one pan]
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 onion, sliced
  - 1 red pepper, sliced
  - 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  - 1 tsp ground cumin
  - 1 tsp sweet paprika
  - ½ tsp chilli flakes
  - 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
  - 1 tsp sugar
  - 4–5 eggs
  - "To serve:"
  - Feta, crumbled
  - Coriander or parsley
  - Crusty bread
---

## Method

1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the onion and pepper with a pinch of salt and cook until soft and sweet, about 10 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and spices and stir for a minute until fragrant.
3. Tip in the tomatoes and sugar. Simmer for 10 minutes until thick enough that a spoon leaves a trail. Season.
4. Make small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low for 5–8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still wobble.
5. Scatter over feta and herbs. Serve straight from the pan.

## Notes

> Add a spoon of harissa with the spices if you want more heat.
