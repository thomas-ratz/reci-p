---
title: Banana bread
description: For the bananas that went too far. Moist, not too sweet.
time: 1 h 15 min
serves: 1 loaf
tags: [baking, sweet]
ingredients:
  - "Wet:"
  - 3 very ripe bananas
  - 75 g butter, melted
  - 100 g brown sugar
  - 1 egg
  - 1 tsp vanilla
  - "Dry:"
  - 190 g plain flour
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - ½ tsp salt
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - 60 g walnuts, chopped (optional)
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a loaf tin with baking paper.
2. Mash the bananas in a big bowl. Stir in the melted butter, then the sugar, egg and vanilla.
3. Sprinkle the dry ingredients over and fold until just combined — a few streaks of flour are fine. Fold in the walnuts.
4. Pour into the tin and bake for 55–65 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
5. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then on a rack.
