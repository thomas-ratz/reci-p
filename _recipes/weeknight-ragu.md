---
title: Weeknight ragù
description: A quick beef and tomato sauce that tastes like it took all afternoon.
time: 50 min
serves: 4
tags: [pasta, dinner]
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 500 g beef mince
  - 1 onion, finely chopped
  - 1 carrot, finely chopped
  - 1 celery stick, finely chopped
  - 2 garlic cloves, grated
  - 2 tbsp tomato paste
  - 150 ml red wine
  - 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
  - 1 bay leaf
  - 400 g rigatoni or tagliatelle
  - Parmesan, to serve
---

## Method

1. Brown the mince in the oil over high heat, in two batches if needed. Don't stir too often — you want a dark crust. Lift out.
2. Lower the heat, add onion, carrot and celery and cook for 8 minutes until soft.
3. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes until the paste darkens.
4. Pour in the wine and scrape up the bits on the bottom. Let it bubble down by half.
5. Return the beef, add tomatoes, bay leaf and a splash of water. Simmer gently for 25 minutes. Season.
6. Cook the pasta, keeping a mug of pasta water. Toss pasta with the sauce, loosening with pasta water. Serve with parmesan.
